How the numbers are made

Every mission is solved before playback by a flight-dynamics engine written for this page: real launch-vehicle data with a gravity-turn ascent through the US-1976 atmosphere, finite burns with the rocket equation, Earth–Moon–Sun n-body coasting, patched conics and Lambert solutions between planets, B-plane targeting for flybys, and lifting entries with heating and parachutes. Planet positions use JPL approximate ephemerides; the Moon uses a truncated ELP/Meeus series; body orientations follow the IAU WGCCRE models.
