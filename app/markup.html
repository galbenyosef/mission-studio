How the numbers are made

Every mission is solved before playback by a flight-dynamics engine written for this page: real launch-vehicle data with a gravity-turn ascent through the US-1976 atmosphere, finite burns with the rocket equation, Earth–Moon–Sun n-body coasting, patched conics and Lambert solutions between planets, B-plane targeting for flybys, and lifting entries with heating and parachutes. Space Shuttle flights add J2 (Earth oblateness) to every orbit, phasing burns timed to the historical docking, guided winged entries with bank steering and roll reversals, and the approach around the heading alignment cone onto the runway. Planet positions use JPL approximate ephemerides; the Moon uses a truncated ELP/Meeus series; body orientations follow the IAU WGCCRE models.
